About us

A library for learning, research and innovation

The University of Matobo Library is committed to providing equitable access to quality information resources, digital technologies and professional services. We support teaching, learning, research and innovation, with a distinctive focus on sustainable drylands agriculture and societal development.

Our vision: To be a leading, inclusive and digitally enabled academic library advancing excellence in teaching, learning, research and innovation.

Our mission

To provide equitable access to quality information resources, digital technologies and professional library services that support the University community and advance knowledge for sustainable development.
